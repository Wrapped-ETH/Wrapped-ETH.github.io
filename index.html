What is Wrapped ETH?

WETH is ETH held in a smart contract and represented as an ERC-20 token for Ethereum apps. ethereum.org explains that deposits mint WETH and redemptions burn WETH for ETH.

How Wrapped ETH works

  1. Connect walletOpen the app, connect an Ethereum wallet, and confirm you are on the network you intend to use.
  2. Choose directionSelect wrap to deposit ETH for WETH, or unwrap to redeem WETH back into native ETH.
  3. Confirm in walletReview the amount and gas cost, then approve the transaction in your wallet.
  4. Verify before sendingUse the correct chain and contract address before moving WETH into DeFi, NFT, or exchange workflows.

Before you wrap

ERC-20 compatibility

ETH predates ERC-20, while WETH gives wallets, DEXs, lending markets, and NFT apps a standard token interface.

Contract-based redemption

The mainnet WETH9 contract exposes deposit and withdraw functions, with the verified token page available on Etherscan.

Network awareness

WETH can exist at different addresses on different chains, so users should verify the network and token contract before transfers.

Wrapped ETH FAQ

What is wrapped eth?

Wrapped eth is WETH, an ERC-20 representation of ETH created by depositing ETH into the WETH smart contract and redeemable back for ETH.

How do I get wrapped eth?

Open the app, connect a wallet, choose wrap, enter an ETH amount, and confirm the transaction; keep some native ETH for gas.

Is wrapped eth the same price as ETH?

At the contract level, WETH is minted and redeemed 1:1 with ETH, though market quotes can vary slightly across venues.

Can wrapped eth pay Ethereum gas?

No. Gas on Ethereum is paid in native ETH, so do not wrap your entire balance if you need to make more transactions.

Which wrapped eth contract should I verify?

On Ethereum mainnet, verify 0xC02aaA39b223FE8D0A0e5C4F27eAD9083C756Cc2 on Etherscan; other networks can use different WETH addresses.
